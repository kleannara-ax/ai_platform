1. General

The actual Base Service Charge(ABSC) payable in any period (p) of year (y) during the Contracted Service Period shall be calculated by multiplying the relevant Base Service Charge, as stated in the Appendix 8, by an adjustment factor (AFBSCpy) calculated in accordance with the provisions of parapgraph 8.2 in this Appendix 8. Hence:
ABSCpy = BSC × AFBSCpy
